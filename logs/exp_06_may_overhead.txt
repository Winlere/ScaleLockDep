--- What the Experiment Reveals ---

1. Under high contention, the application is already serialized by the
   shared mutex; adding lockdep roughly doubles cost per pair (2.0–2.4x
   across the sweep) regardless of mode. global and rb track each other
   within 5%; the wait-chain check fires in both, and the per-acquire
   bookkeeping is the same order of magnitude.

2. Under low contention, the absolute overhead is smaller (1.5–1.8x) and
   shrinks as thread count grows. The per-thread mutex means TLS-cache
   lookups and held-stack pushes/pops are the dominant cost, not
   cycle-detection work — and those scale with parallelism on the same
   trajectory as baseline pthread.

3. global vs rb is nearly indistinguishable in this benchmark because
   nesting depth never exceeds 1 — there are no held locks when each
   acquire happens, so the O(depth) work that rb is designed to offload
   does not exist. rb costs the enqueue, gains nothing. The pedges and
   nesting experiments (where depth varies) are where the two backends
   diverge.

4. The 1-thread high-contention row (2.1x) is pure interception cost
   (dlsym indirection + recursive guard + held-stack push/pop). This is
   the floor of lockdep overhead for any workload.
