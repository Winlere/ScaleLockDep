--- What the Experiment Reveals ---

1. High contention overhead is now 1.7–2.6× across all thread counts,
   compared to 4.2–12.2× previously. The catastrophic 12.2× at 64 threads
   is eliminated — overhead is flat at ~2.2× for ≥32 threads.

2. Low contention overhead is the headline result: the previous
   implementation collapsed from 4× at 1 thread to 592× at 64 threads.
   The per-thread RB implementation holds at 1.5–2.1× across all thread
   counts. The 592× → 1.7× improvement at 64 threads is a 348× reduction
   in overhead ratio.

3. Lockdep now scales with thread count in the low-contention scenario:
   34.8M ops/s at 1 thread → 60.8M at 64 threads. The previous
   implementation went from 17.4M → 0.2M (anti-scaling). This confirms
   that the per-thread RB backend eliminates the g_meta_lock serialization
   that was the root cause of the scalability failure.

4. At 1 thread (uncontended), lockdep throughput doubled: 17.7M → 34.7M
   ops/s. The per-operation fixed cost dropped from ~56 ns to ~29 ns,
   matching the latency experiment findings.
