Baseline scales (lock operations are parallel). Lockdep collapses — even
though there's no application-level contention, every lock/unlock
funnels through g_meta_lock, making lockdep's internal state update
fully serialized. This is the scalability bottleneck identified by the
experiment.

---
What the experiment reveals

The single global spinlock (g_meta_lock in state.c) protecting the
dependency graph and slot registries is the root cause. When threads
don't contend on application locks at all, lockdep's tracking overhead
still serializes them completely — overhead scales with thread count,
not with actual lock contention. This is the primary target for a
ScaleLockDep improvement (e.g., per-lock or sharded metadata locks, or
read-write locks for the dependency graph reads).
