--- What the Experiment Reveals ---

1. The per-thread RB implementation (commit f760bcd) dramatically reduces
   overhead in the lock-thrashing region compared to the previous commit
   (9295d08):

   - At cs_ns=0: overhead dropped from 3.8× to 2.0× (1.9× improvement)
   - At cs_ns=10: 3.8× → 1.8× (2.1× improvement)
   - At cs_ns=20: 3.1× → 1.4× (2.2× improvement)
   - At cs_ns=30: 2.6× → 1.2× (2.2× improvement)

2. Lockdep throughput at cs_ns=0 nearly doubled: 3.2M → 6.8M ops/s. This
   confirms the per-thread RB backend significantly reduces metadata
   contention on the hot path.

3. The crossover to ≤1.5× overhead now happens at ~20 ns (was ~75 ns in
   Mar 24). Near-unity overhead is reached at ~30 ns (was ~200 ns). This
   means the new implementation is effectively invisible for any workload
   with critical sections longer than ~30 ns.

4. In the work-dominated region (cs_ns ≥ 200), overhead is consistently
   ≤1.0×, with lockdep sometimes appearing faster than baseline (within
   measurement noise). This matches the Mar 24 behavior — once application
   serialization dominates, lockdep cost is negligible.
